# Scarlet Refrain boss theme

`flandre_theme.MID` was provided by the user for the hidden boss fight.
The original file is preserved; its MIDI arrangement/source credits have not
been supplied. The music retains its upstream rights.

On Windows, the game uses its own MCI MIDI sequencer and the system MIDI
synthesizer. No extra package, audio conversion, or downloaded soundfont is
required. The theme begins at the supplied pre-fight dialogue cue (before
"I have a play toy over here..."), continues into combat, loops at its end, pauses with
the game, and stops when the fight ends, restarts, or the window is hidden or
closed. It stops before the post-fight conversation. Press **M** to mute/unmute
both music and sound effects without losing the current music position.

This sequencer is independent of the main music player's media and settings.
Playback failures leave the game playable and show a Japanese BGM status.
