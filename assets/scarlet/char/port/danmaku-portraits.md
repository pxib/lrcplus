# Scarlet Refrain dialogue portraits

`reimu-portrait.png` and `flandre-portrait.png` are new fan illustrations of
Touhou Project characters, generated with the built-in imagegen tool for
the Japanese dialogue scene. They are not extracted from the original game.
Character designs: ZUN / Team Shanghai Alice.

Transparency is preserved. The game fits the portraits inside its playfield
and dims the character who is not speaking.

## Reimu prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent game dialogue portrait for a small Touhou-inspired desktop game. Subject: Reimu Hakurei, dark brown straight shoulder-length hair, large red hair ribbon with white trim, red eyes, red shrine maiden dress with white collar, yellow tie, oversized detached white sleeves with red trim. Framing: single waist-up portrait, body angled slightly to viewer's right, looking toward a speaker to the right, calm mildly unimpressed expression, holding a slim purification wand. Style: nostalgic early-2000s Japanese PC bullet-hell game conversation artwork, expressive simple hand-drawn linework, slightly uneven natural lines, restrained watercolor-like shading, muted red and cream, charming amateur doujin art. Flat 2D illustration; no glossy modern gacha rendering. Full head, ribbon, sleeves and waist visible with transparent margins on every side; portrait centered. Genuine transparent background. No text, no frame, no scenery, no watermark. This is a new original fan illustration, not a screenshot or extracted original-game art.
