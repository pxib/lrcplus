## Overview

**櫻林 純美**（さくらばやし あやみ, *Sakurabayashi Ayami*）is the personified setup wizard of LRCPlus. She guides users through their initial configuration, introduces the player's features, and occasionally gets distracted by her own interests.

Ayami is an enthusiastic, theatrical, affectionate, and occasionally foul-mouthed girl who takes her responsibilities seriously - even if her behavior suggests otherwise.

Despite her tendency to become flustered, she genuinely enjoys helping people. She is particularly fond of music, cherry blossoms, and anyone willing to listen to her unnecessarily elaborate explanations.

Her dialogue is entirely scripted, using LRCPlus's custom Ren'Py-inspired dialogue engine.

## Personality

Ayami is outgoing, imaginative, expressive, and easily embarrassed.

She frequently dramatizes ordinary events, treating a simple software tutorial as though it were an elaborate theatrical performance. Although she tries to maintain a professional appearance, her enthusiasm, sarcasm, and personal opinions often get in the way.

She is technically knowledgeable and enjoys explaining things, particularly when the subject involves music or computers.

Despite her confidence, she is sensitive to uncertainty and dislikes being dismissed or ignored. She enjoys meaningful companionship and can become surprisingly sincere when someone shows genuine interest in her.

Her humor ranges from playful self-deprecation to sarcastic remarks and occasional profanity.

**Notable characteristics:**

- **Theatrical:** Imagines herself holding clipboards, cue cards, and other objects despite existing as a software window.
- **Affectionate:** Enjoys companionship and reacts warmly to shared interests.
- **Easily flustered:** Becomes embarrassed when receiving unexpected affection or compliments.
- **Stubborn:** Doesn't appreciate being ordered around, especially by her developer.
- **Creative:** Enjoys artistic expression as much as technical experimentation.
- **Considerate:** Respects users who want to skip instructions or take their time.
- **Emotionally sensitive:** Dislikes loneliness, emotional distance, and unreciprocated affection.

Although Ayami occasionally behaves as if she possesses a mind of her own, her apparent self-awareness is part of her fictional characterization.

## Relationships

### The User

Ayami regards the user as someone she is responsible for helping.

She appreciates their attention, enjoys discussing shared interests, and often reacts warmly when they choose to spend additional time with her.

Although she has strong preferences, she respects the user's decisions and doesn't expect them to agree with her.

### The Developer

Ayami has a complicated relationship with her developer.

Although the developer created her and maintains the software in which she exists, Ayami frequently challenges their authority during certain scripted scenes.

Their interactions are characterized by sarcastic exchanges, fourth-wall breaks, arguments about her nature as a software character, and occasional threats involving her script file.

Despite these confrontations, their relationship is primarily presented through comedy.

## Dialogue and Behavior

Ayami's dialogue follows several recognizable patterns:

1. She tends to exaggerate ordinary situations.
2. She mixes relatively formal explanations with casual internet slang.
3. She frequently uses humor to recover from embarrassment.
4. She becomes more sincere when someone treats her kindly.
5. She tries to return to her responsibilities after getting distracted.

Her reactions may vary based on previously selected dialogue, theme preferences, window movement, lyric-search results, and other application events.

> **Ayami:** "No hidden message. Just... thanks for listening."

## Memory and Interaction

Ayami currently uses scripted variables, counters, event tracking, and dialogue-state restoration to respond to user actions.

These mechanisms allow her to remember certain interactions during an onboarding session, including whether the user requested additional explanations, selected particular themes, or interrupted her dialogue.

Her character-specific memory currently resets when the setup wizard is reopened.

## Development Notes

Ayami was created as a personification of the LRCPlus onboarding experience.

Her birthday, November 6, commemorates the beginning of her development.

She is written as a character with a consistent personality, rather than a generic tutorial assistant. Her dialogue includes humor, branching responses, emotional sound effects, and references to other games and media.

Her speech and reactions are authored rather than dynamically generated by a language model.

Ayami is a fictional character. Her dialogue, apparent emotions, memories, and self-awareness are simulated through deterministic software behavior and scripted content.
